EVENT RULES

A safe, respectful and memorable event for everyone.

01 · NO SMOKING OR VAPING

Smoking, cigarettes, vapes and e-cigarettes are not permitted at the event or inside the venue.

02 · TICKETS & ENTRY

A valid ticket is required. Each ticket permits one entry. Do not share, duplicate or resell tickets without authorisation.

03 · NO REFUNDS

Ticket sales are final, except where the event is cancelled or rescheduled under the announced policy and applicable law.

04 · RESPECT THE CROWD

No harassment, unwanted touching, discrimination, intimidation or violence. Respect everyone's boundaries.

05 · ZERO TOLERANCE

Weapons, illegal drugs and dangerous or prohibited substances are forbidden. Unsafe or illegal behaviour may result in removal.

06 · NO BAD VIBES

Fighting, threats, property damage or deliberately disrupting the event will not be tolerated.

07 · FOLLOW THE CREW

Follow event staff and security instructions. Entry may be refused or guests removed for rule violations, subject to applicable law.

08 · VENUE & SAFETY

Respect venue property, emergency exits and safety instructions. Never block entrances or exits.

09 · PHONES & RECORDING

Do not record or share private moments without consent. Follow venue photography restrictions.

10 · NO RE-ENTRY

Re-entry is not permitted unless explicitly allowed by event staff.

11 · RESPONSIBLE RAVING

Look out for your group. If anyone feels unwell or unsafe, contact event staff immediately.

12 · THE FINAL WORD

By entering the WLR Productions event, guests agree to follow event rules and applicable venue policies.

The event organiser must publish the confirmed age-entry policy, accessibility and emergency contact information before ticket sales begin.
